Just like any commercial gaming platform, a company has many
incentives to replace recognizable units of value like dollars and
cents with some fiat that allows them to arbitrarily change the value
at will.  With LLMs it gets worse.  Not only do they arbitrarily
define tokens as having a certain value, that value will vary among
multiple models published by the same company.  It may change between
versions of the same model.  It feels nice when the old models get
"cheaper", but it's all an illusion.  Especially since they are also
in control of the tokenization process.  As Tom Waits<sup>*</sup> says in *Step
Right Up* ["The large print giveth, the small print taketh
away."](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kTdScE3Rqh8)
